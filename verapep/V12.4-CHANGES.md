# VERAPEP v12.4 — colour-coded database-name vials

- Replaced the BPC-labelled physical base with a neutral photorealistic glass vial.
- Every vial label now renders the catalogue/database `product.name` directly.
- Strength is rendered from the product's available variant specification.
- Vial cap, label band and liquid colour are driven by the product's existing `discoveryGoals`.
- The same vial renderer is used in homepage cards, catalogue cards, product detail and cart.
- No backend/database schema or catalogue records were changed.
