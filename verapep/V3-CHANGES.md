# VERAPEP v3 changes

## Customer interface
- Guided, clickable catalogue filters.
- Category, minimum-rating and lab-report filters.
- Visible active-filter chips and accurate result counts.
- Stable rating component that prevents broken star/score wrapping.
- "No ratings yet" state instead of a misleading zero score.
- Saved products shortlist stored locally for guests.
- Product comparison for up to three catalogue entries.
- Embedded Ask Vera panel on the homepage.
- Ask Vera quick actions can reduce the visible catalogue immediately.
- Full support page includes direct catalogue-filter shortcuts.
- Product pages include save, compare and Ask Vera actions.
- Approved reviews are displayed on product pages; new reviews remain moderated.

## Administration
- Review status filtering.
- Review summary metrics: submitted, pending, published and public average.
- Approved reviews alone affect public ratings and rating filters.
- Product content, delivery, lab reports and publication remain controlled in admin.
- AI knowledge and fallback responses remain controlled in admin.

## Commerce safety
- All catalogue entries remain information-only.
- No cart or checkout button is shown for unapproved products.
- Saved products replaces ambiguous "inbox" terminology.
