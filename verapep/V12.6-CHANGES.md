# VERAPEP v12.6 — Homepage visual QA

This release is a presentation-only QA correction based on the selected homepage direction.

## Fixed
- Restored the complete photorealistic glass-vial body in every product card.
- Product vial labels are generated from catalogue product names and variant strengths.
- Natural, deterministic line breaks replace arbitrary mid-word wrapping.
- Goal colours are consistent between product vials and the seven homepage goal controls.
- Multi-goal products use one dominant colour with a small secondary accent instead of a full mixed gradient.
- Presentation-only punctuation and spacing cleanup for visibly malformed catalogue names; stored database values are unchanged.
- Corrected the visible `Whitout` typo to `Without` on the homepage only.
- Normalised units to mg, µg, IU and mL.
- Reviewed hero bubbles and all three homepage information-card illustrations for palette consistency.

- 0 products missing a goal colour.
- 20/20 Node regression tests passed.
