# VERAPEP V4 changes

## Homepage and discovery

- Replaced the generic category-first experience with a three-step VERAPEP product finder:
  1. broad customer interest,
  2. a more specific information area,
  3. optional documentation and presentation priorities.
- Added seven focus areas and 34 specific information-area labels mapped to the existing 84 catalogue products.
- Results update in the same visible catalogue instead of opening a separate recommendation list.
- Added active filter chips, live product count, relevance sorting and clear reset controls.
- Kept the four original catalogue categories for users who prefer traditional browsing.

## Product cards, ratings and saved products

- Added a visible heart button at the top-right of each product card.
- Saved products remain separate from the future cart and persist in the browser.
- Added a full Saved products section to My Pages.
- Rebuilt the rating component so stars, score and review count remain grouped and wrap cleanly.
- Products without approved reviews display “No ratings yet”.
- Added clear information-only, lab-report and specialist badges.

## Product pages

- Reworked product pages into a vertical information flow.
- Added a visual product panel, stable ratings, Save, Compare and Ask Vera actions.
- Added vertical accordion sections for overview, information areas, ingredients/contents, storage, usage information, warnings, delivery, European availability and lab reports.
- Retained the source-workbook variant rows, with clearer catalogue-only language.
- Added a mobile sticky action bar.

## Ask Vera

- The floating Ask Vera panel can apply the same focus and priority filters used by the homepage.
- The full support page links directly to goal-filtered catalogue views.
- The assistant still answers only from admin-approved knowledge and escalates unknown questions.

## Visual experience

- Added a truthful trust-signal bar controlled through Store settings.
- The trust bar is static on desktop and rotates slowly on mobile.
- Added a compact sticky header, reveal-on-scroll transitions and subtle CSS background bubbles.
- Motion is disabled when the user prefers reduced motion.

## Administration

- Added product-level fields for customer focus tags, specific information-area tags, ingredients/contents, specialist status and information completeness.
- Added editable trust signals and assistant name under Store settings.
- Added a taxonomy preview under Guide & AI.
- Existing review moderation, lab reports, product visibility and catalogue-only controls remain in place.

## Safety status

- All products remain information-only.
- Live commerce, product approval, final EUR pricing and country eligibility remain disabled.
- Product-finder results are described as related catalogue information, not medical recommendations.
