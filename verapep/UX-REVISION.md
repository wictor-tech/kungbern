# UX revision

Updated customer-facing language and layout after a full heuristic review.

## Storefront
- Simplified navigation and hero messaging.
- Replaced the large QA section with a compact demo notice.
- Reduced the browsing explanation to three steps.
- Removed direct quick-add from catalogue cards; customers open a product and choose a variant first.
- Reduced mobile initial catalogue display from 12 to 6 products.
- Improved long-name wrapping and product-card hierarchy.
- Removed admin links from customer-facing footers.

## Cart and checkout
- Replaced internal QA language with customer-facing demo language.
- Combined two similar checkout confirmations into one statement.
- Clarified tax and order-total labels.
- Reduced heading scale and improved cart/checkout hierarchy.

## Administration
- Reframed the page as an operations dashboard.
- Improved dashboard grouping, statistic cards, sticky tabs and table readability.
- Simplified labels for orders, inventory, product access, returns and messages.
- Improved mobile spacing and responsive layout.
