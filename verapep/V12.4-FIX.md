# VERAPEP v12.4 — vial identity and colour fix

Frontend-only update.

- Removed the baked-in BPC-157 label from the common vial base.
- Every vial now renders its product display name and first/preferred strength dynamically from storefront/database data.
- Vial accent/liquid colour is driven by the product's existing `discoveryGoals` metadata.
- Seven catalogue colour families match the seven homepage goal areas: Weight & metabolism, Skin appearance, Strength & recovery, Energy, Sleep & focus, Healthy ageing, Hormonal & specialist.
- Products with multiple discovery goals use the primary goal colour with the second goal blended into the cap/label band.
- Card labels were enlarged for readability.
- Backend, database, catalogue source and product-content source are unchanged.
